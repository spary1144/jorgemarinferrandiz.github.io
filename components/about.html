About me

A programmer who enjoys the meeting point between player experience and production reality.

I like roles where I can help both the game and the people making it: gameplay that feels better, tools that save time and audio that supports the mood early.

How I work

I graduated in Multimedia Engineering in 2023 and later completed U-tad's Advanced Master's Degree in Video Game Programming in late 2025. Since 2020, I have kept building projects to deepen my understanding of the technical side of game development.

The work I enjoy most usually sits between gameplay feel, systems design and practical tooling. I like solving the player-facing problem and the team-facing problem together whenever possible.

Outside programming, I spend time on sound design and composition, often as part of prototypes where audio helps define the identity of the experience from the start.

What I bring

  • Ownership over features from technical design to implementation details.
  • A strong bridge between designer intent and code structure.
  • Tooling and debugging instincts that improve day-to-day iteration.
  • Audio awareness that supports impact, readability and atmosphere.
